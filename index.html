Core Philosophy

Care. Play.
Learning. Growth.

Every child is born curious. At UgyenPee ECCD, we create the gentle rhythm, safe space, and nurturing relationships that allow young children to blossom naturally through play, discovery, and shared joy.

Located in peaceful Hejo near the Indian Embassy in Thimphu, our centre is designed to be a trusted second home where little learners feel secure, valued, and encouraged every day.

Holistic Curriculum

Six areas of a
growing child.

Guided by Bhutan's early childhood care standards and child-centred pedagogy, ensuring children build well-rounded confidence.

01

Physical Development

Gross and fine motor skills, body coordination, balance, and joyful outdoor physical games.

02

Social & Emotional

Empathy, sharing, emotional self-regulation, building friendships, and practicing kindness.

03

Language & Communication

Storytelling, expressive vocabulary, receptive listening, conversational ease, and rhymes.

04

Cognitive & Problem Solving

Early mathematical thinking, curiosity, classification, sorting, puzzles, and reasoning.

05

Creative Expression

Art, finger painting, music, traditional Bhutanese rhythms, dramatic costume play, and crafts.

06

Exploration & Discovery

Nature awareness, asking questions, sensory gardening, observing plants, and science through play.
