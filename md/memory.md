# UgyenPee ECCD Website
## Project Memory

**Project:** UgyenPee Early Childhood Care and Development Public Website  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** September 2026

---

## 1. Project Purpose

Develop a modern public-facing website for UgyenPee ECCD in Thimphu, Bhutan.

The website will serve as the organisation's official digital information platform for parents, guardians, community members and stakeholders.

---

## 2. Core Product

This is initially a:

> **Public ECCD information and communication website.**

It is NOT initially:
- a parent portal
- student management system
- LMS
- payment system
- attendance system
- internal administration system

---

## 3. Target Audience

Primary:
- Parents
- Guardians
- Prospective families

Secondary:
- Existing parents
- Community
- Education professionals
- Government/sector stakeholders
- Partners

---

## 4. Core Message

The website should communicate:

> A safe, caring and joyful environment where children learn, play, explore and grow.

Exact wording must be validated with UgyenPee.

---

## 5. Content Rules

### Rule 1
Never invent organisation facts.

### Rule 2
Anything not confirmed by UgyenPee should be marked:

`TBC — To Be Confirmed`

### Rule 3
Use real photographs wherever possible.

### Rule 4
Obtain appropriate permission before publishing identifiable images of children.

### Rule 5
Do not expose private child information.

---

## 6. Known Public Research

Potentially relevant public information identified during research:

- UgyenPee ECCD is publicly listed in Hejo, Thimphu, near the Indian Embassy.
- A public phone listing associates the centre with +975 17703534.
- The centre has limited public digital information compared with the information needed for a full official website.
- Bhutan's ECCD ecosystem emphasises holistic child development, play, wellbeing and family participation.
- Bhutan's Ministry of Education and Skills Development has published guidelines for private ECCD centres.
- UNICEF Bhutan provides ECCD and parenting resources.
- Bhutan's private ECCD sector continues to develop and organise professionally.

All organisation-specific details must be verified before publication.

## 10. Design Memory

Desired visual identity:

- Warm
- Natural
- Professional
- Child-centred
- Modern
- Bhutanese
- Trustworthy

Avoid:
- Excessive cartoon graphics
- Generic daycare templates
- Overly corporate layouts
- Excessive animation
- Stock photography wherever authentic photos are available

## 14. Development Principles

### Components
Build reusable components.

### Content
Keep content separate from presentation where practical.

### Images
Optimise all images.

### Accessibility
Treat accessibility as a requirement, not a final polish step.

### Responsive
Design mobile-first.

### Performance
Avoid unnecessary libraries and heavy animation.

### Maintainability
Keep naming and file structure predictable.
