# UgyenPee ECCD Public Website
## Business Requirements Document (BRD)

**Project:** UgyenPee Early Childhood Care and Development (ECCD) Public Website  
**Organisation:** UgyenPee ECCD, Thimphu, Bhutan  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft for client validation  
**Date:** September 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary

UgyenPee ECCD requires a modern public-facing website that establishes a clear and trustworthy digital presence for parents, guardians, prospective families, community members, and partners.

Current public information about UgyenPee ECCD appears limited and fragmented across online directory/social platforms. The website should therefore become the organisation's primary source of official information.

The website will communicate UgyenPee ECCD's identity, philosophy, learning environment, programmes, activities, facilities, admission information, parent resources, announcements, and contact details.

The website is a **public information and communication platform**. It is not intended to be a full student-management system, learning-management system, or parent portal in the first release.

---

## 2. Background

Early Childhood Care and Development in Bhutan focuses on children's holistic development, learning through play, nurturing relationships, wellbeing, and family/community participation.

Relevant Bhutanese ECCD references include:
- Ministry of Education and Skills Development guidelines for private ECCD centres.
- UNICEF Bhutan ECCD research and resources.
- Bhutanese ECCD organisations and learning institutions.
- Current developments in Bhutan's private ECCD sector.

The website should reflect the Bhutanese ECCD context while presenting UgyenPee as a contemporary, trustworthy and child-centred organisation.

## 9. Business Requirements

### BR-01: Official Identity
The website must clearly identify UgyenPee ECCD and display the official logo, name and approved brand elements.

### BR-02: Public Information
Visitors must be able to understand the organisation without requiring an account.

### BR-03: Parent-Centred Information
Admission, programme, safety, location and contact information must be easy to access.

### BR-04: Authentic Representation
Images and claims should represent the actual UgyenPee ECCD environment.

### BR-05: Content Accuracy
Unverified information must not be published.

### BR-06: Mobile Accessibility
The website must work well on smartphones, tablets and desktops.

### BR-07: Contactability
Visitors must be able to call, email or locate the centre easily.

### BR-08: Discoverability
The website should use appropriate metadata, page titles, descriptions and structured content for search engines.

### BR-09: Social Integration
Official social media channels may be linked or embedded where appropriate.

### BR-10: Future Expansion
The architecture should allow future addition of parent portals, online admissions or other services without redesigning the entire public site.

## 11. Privacy and Child Protection

Because the website concerns young children, privacy must be treated as a high-priority requirement.

The project must include:
- Parent/guardian consent before publishing identifiable child photographs.
- No unnecessary personal information about children.
- No public child records.
- No public phone numbers or sensitive information of children.
- Careful handling of staff personal information.
- Appropriate image alt text without identifying children unnecessarily.
- Secure hosting and content management practices.
